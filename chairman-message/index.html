Chairman’s message

CA SHRI SHRIPAD D. KHIRE

Chairman

It gives me immense pleasure to convey my warm greetings to all our valued customers, shareholders, and well-wishers. Sangli Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd. has been serving society with a strong commitment towards trust, transparency, and customer satisfaction since its inception.

Our bank has consistently focused on inclusive growth by supporting small businesses, entrepreneurs, farmers, and individuals from all sections of society. With a strong foundation laid by our founders and the continued guidance of our board of directors, the bank has achieved steady progress while maintaining financial discipline and regulatory compliance.

In today’s rapidly changing banking environment, we are moving confidently towards modern technology and digital banking solutions while preserving the cooperative values that define our identity. Our aim is to provide secure, efficient, and customer-friendly banking services across all our branches.

I sincerely thank our customers for their continued trust and support. I also appreciate the dedicated efforts of our staff, management, and stakeholders who contribute tirelessly to the bank’s success. Together, we look forward to a future of sustainable growth and excellence.

CA SHRI SHRIPAD D. KHIRE
Chairman
